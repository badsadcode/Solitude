# Solitude

*A submarine captain, alone, on the floor of an alien ocean.*

A first-person submarine survival-horror game built in **Godot 4.7.2 (C#, Forward+)** with a
PS1-era look: low-res rendering, vertex jitter, dithering, CRT camera feeds, and a fully
synthesized soundscape designed to make you uncomfortable about what's below you.

A [Bad Ideas Works](https://github.com/badsadcode) production.

## Current state — Milestone 5: the hard suit

- Suit up at the **dive locker** (engineering), climb to the **dive hatch** with the boat stopped,
  and you're outside: swim where you look, ~4 minutes of air, helmet lamp, a homing chevron back to
  the hatch, your own breathing in your ears and everything else muffled through the glass.
- Pick things up by hand (six-slot sample bag, stowed in the hold when you get back).
- The ravager likes divers. The first passes only look.

### Creatures (models by Tom, animated procedurally on their bones)

| Creature | Where | Temperament |
|---|---|---|
| **Ravager** (15 m) | everywhere, out at the edge of your lights | circles the boat, crosses the bow, stalks divers |
| **Abyssal Crystal Ray** (9 m span) | groups over the glowing meadows | harmless; curious about your lights, circles, drifts off |
| **Abyssal Fangwyrm** (40 m) | only once you've lingered below ~520 m | apex: circles far out, long passes, may drag itself along your hull |

New creatures plug in through `Scripts/Creatures/CreatureProfile.cs`: name the bones that wave, flap
or open, give a size and a temperament. Debug: **F8** rays, **F9** ravager pass, **F10** Fangwyrm pass.

## Milestone 4: deep-sea stations

- **Stations** scattered across the seabed, each with a **distress beacon** you can hear (and see on
  sonar and the holo table). The first one is always a few hundred metres from where you start.
- **Docking**: follow the lit approach lane into the berth; the helm shows distance, lateral and
  vertical offset and heading error; the lane goes green when you're lined up. In the capture zone,
  **K** engages magnetic clamps that pull the boat in. **K** again undocks.
- **Aboard**: climb the dive hatch in engineering. Interiors are generated by
  [NoorLevel](docs/NOORLEVEL_INTEGRATION.md) (research facilities and survey outposts), restyled and
  filled at runtime: powered or dead (bring your **flashlight**), keycard bulkheads, secret rooms,
  crew logs that get worse the more you read, loot (food, medkits, materials, ammo, weapons), and the
  **core** with a **map fragment** that charts the seabed and plots other stations.
- **Crawlers**: many-legged things that nest in dead corridors. They hear gunshots and running,
  hunt you along a navmesh, and lunge. **Pistol**, **assault rifle**, **shotgun**.
- Stations remember what you took and what you killed.

## Milestone 3: survival systems

- **Manipulator arm** — operate it from the bridge's port console: two-bone IK hydraulic arm with its
  own camera and work light. Mouse steers the claw, wheel / W-S height, click grabs. Catches are
  folded back and stowed in the hold.
- **Resources** on the seabed (remembered once taken): manganese nodules, lattice crystals, lumen
  pods, vent clams. The **intake filter** (G at the helm) gathers plankton, and sediment near the bottom.
- **Workbench** (plates, sealant, circuits, hull patch kits, power cells), **galley** (rations, clam
  stew, glow broth — and eating), **engineering panel** (install power cells, weld hull patches).
- **Power**: engine, floods, sonar, arm and intake all drain the battery; recharge with power cells
  or by resting near a hydrothermal vent. At zero: emergency bus, crippled engine, dark floods.
- **Hunger**: eat or your hands start shaking on the controls.

## Milestone 2: sonar and holo table

- **Active sonar** — ping (P anywhere at the helm, or at the console) and 360 beams sweep the real
  seabed with **acoustic shadowing**: what hides behind a ridge stays unknown. Heading-up PPI scope
  with a travelling wavefront, phosphor-fading returns, amber for terrain at or above your keel,
  three ranges, echoes you can hear coming back off nearby walls. Sometimes something answers
  that isn't the seabed.
- **Holo table** — a wireframe projection of everything the sonar has ever seen (persistent map
  memory), revealed in step with the wavefront. Orbit it with the mouse, zoom with the wheel.

- **First creature: the ravager** — a 15 m predator (model by Tom) animated procedurally: tail wave,
  turning curl, gaping jaw, eyes that glow in the dark. It circles out of sight and sometimes crosses
  your bow. It answers sonar. **F9** (debug) summons a pass.

## Milestone 1: the vertical slice

- **Walkable interior** — bridge, sensor bay, galley, quarters/workshop, engineering. The room
  leans as the hull pitches and rolls; you drift downhill and feel engine vibration and impacts.
- **Helm** with semi-realistic hydrodynamics: engine spool-up, anisotropic drag, rudder and dive
  planes that only bite with water flow, slow ballast tanks, self-righting roll.
  Two drive modes: **Telegraph** (ring engine orders) and **Hold-to-run**.
  Views: bridge screen / bow camera / external drone.
- **Camera monitor station** — six real hull cameras (bow, port, starboard, keel, stern, panning
  mast) rendered live into CRT screens, with signal dropouts. Any feed to the big screen or fullscreen.
- **Procedural, streamed seabed** — canyons, ridged mountains, terraced cliffs, shelf drop-offs into
  the abyss, spires, bioluminescent meadows, hydrothermal vents, and the occasional carcass of
  something enormous.
- **Atmosphere** — marine snow, volumetric fog lit by your floodlights, depth-driven darkness,
  PS1 post-processing.
- **Audio** — synthesized in real time on its own thread: abyssal drone, ventilation, engine,
  hull creaks and groans that worsen with depth, and distant calls you won't get an explanation for.

## Running

Open the folder in Godot 4.7.2 (.NET build) and press Play, or:

```
Godot_v4.7.2-stable_mono_win64.exe --path . -- --seed=1234
```

`--seed=N` replays a specific world. Without it every dive is a new ocean.

## Controls

| Context | Keys |
|---|---|
| Walking | **WASD** move · **Shift** run · **E** use · **I** hold · **Esc** release mouse · **F11** fullscreen |
| Arm | mouse claw · wheel / **W-S** height · click grab · **E/Esc** stow |
| Stations (panels) | **1–9** or click an option · **E/Esc** leave |
| Diving | **WASD** swim where you look · **Space/C** up/down · **Shift** boost · **F** lamp · **E** collect / enter airlock |
| Aboard a station | **LMB** fire · **R** reload · **1–3** weapon · **F** flashlight · **H** medkit · **E** use |
| Helm | **W/S** throttle (telegraph steps or hold) · **A/D** + mouse rudder · **R/F** + mouse dive planes · **Space** blow / **C** flood ballast · **P** sonar ping · **G** intake · **K** dock / undock · **X** centre controls · **T** drive mode · **L** floodlights · **V** view · **E** leave |
| Monitors | **1–6** or click a screen · **F** fullscreen feed · **E/Esc** leave |
| Sonar | **Space** / click / **P** ping · **Q** range · **F** fullscreen scope · **E/Esc** leave |
| Holo table | mouse orbit · wheel / **Q** zoom · **Space** / **P** ping · **E/Esc** leave |

## Development notes

- Everything is built in code (`Scripts/Main.cs` is the director); scenes are minimal on purpose.
- **Capture harness:** `-- --capture=<dir>` makes the game drive itself through every mode, save
  screenshots, log telemetry (fps, physics, audio underruns) and record the audio to WAV, then quit.
- 3D assets from other studio tools follow **Noor Interchange** (GLB + `.noor.json`, +Y up, metres).
